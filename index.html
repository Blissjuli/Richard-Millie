Dear Richard —

Some people fill a room; you somehow make it feel safer. We've lost count of the nights that were only meant to be "a quick one" until you sat down — and then suddenly it's midnight, and nobody's bored, and nobody wants to leave.

You have that quiet skill of making people feel properly seen: remembering the small things, sending the right link at the right time, showing up before you're ever asked. That's not a habit. That's just who you are.

So here's another lap around the sun. May this year be everything you've quietly been pointing at — and even the parts none of us can predict, may they turn out better than advertised. We'll be there either way, with candles on standby.
